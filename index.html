The implementation path

Seven steps, in order.

Every stage protects the next one from expensive guessing.

01

Assess

Understand the model, goals, bottlenecks, workflows, systems, data, team, risks and opportunities.

02

Strategise

Map where productivity, creativity, personalisation, automation, intelligence, prediction and agents can help.

03

Prioritise

Choose against ROI, speed, strategic value, team readiness, risk and scalability.

04

Design

Define tools, data access, integrations, permissions, automations, agents, dashboards and governance.

05

Implement

Build quick wins people can feel in their real working day.

06

Train

Build confidence, prompt literacy, responsible use and new workflow behaviour.

07

Scale

Move from isolated use cases to connected systems and agentic execution.

The order matters: assess → strategise → prioritise → design → implement → train → scale.

The seven levels

Move beyond faster emails.

AI value builds like a staircase. You do not need to jump to the top, but you should know which level you are building towards.

1

Productivity

Reduce the everyday friction of drafting, research, meetings, analysis and reporting.

2

Creative empowerment

Expand what each person can create, test and improve without waiting on a blank page.

3

Personalisation

Make customer journeys, follow-up and onboarding feel relevant at scale.

4

Automation

Remove repetitive work so people can focus on judgement, relationships and creativity.

5

Business intelligence

See what is true now through useful dashboards, summaries and operational insight.

6

Prediction

Act earlier on churn, demand, cash flow, buying intent and operational risk.

7

Agentic scale

Direct intelligence, operations and market-outreach agents across structured work.
