On your phone

Add AI FUSION to your phone

Keep AI FUSION on your home screen, like an app. There is nothing to download from an app store. We open in our own window, with the same pages, forms and booking tools you already use.

iPhone, Safari

Tap Share (the square with an up arrow, bottom middle; on newer iOS it may be under the ... menu), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

iPhone, Chrome

Tap Share at the top right, then Add to Home Screen.

Android, Chrome

Tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.

Once the AI FUSION icon is on your home screen, tap it to open us the same way you open any other app.
