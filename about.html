👋 Who we are

AI Fusion was founded by Grant De Swardt, based in East Sussex, England. Grant works with business owners and teams to identify where intelligence can create value, what to build first and how to make the change stick.

The work starts with the business—not a platform. Grant assesses the workflows, systems, data, team, risks and opportunities before recommending technology. If better data, training, ordinary automation or a simpler process is the answer, he will say so.

🤝 How we work

1. Assess — Understand the business model, goals, bottlenecks, workflows, systems, data, team, risks and opportunities.

2. Strategise & Prioritise — Map the opportunities, then choose against ROI, speed, strategic value, readiness, risk and scalability.

3. Design & Implement — Define the infrastructure, permissions and governance, then build the first useful wave for momentum.

4. Train & Scale — Build confidence and responsible workflow behaviour before expanding from isolated wins to connected systems.
