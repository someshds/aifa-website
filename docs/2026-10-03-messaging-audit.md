# AI Fusion website messaging audit

Version 1.0 — 3 October 2026

## Scope and source

The audit covered all 227 HTML documents in the production repository: 112 news pages, 13 blog pages, 51 service pages, 31 root-level pages, 11 product pages, six video pages, one workshop page, one webinar page and one shared include.

The sole source for the replacement positioning was the supplied Transforming Your Work webinar script covering slides 18–37. Existing legal facts, first-party reviews, editorial records, URLs, forms, analytics and integrations were preserved.

## Baseline findings

- The display name “AI Fusion Automations” appeared across 222 pages despite the current AI Fusion naming standard.
- Forty-nine industry pages led with chatbots, automation and “plug-and-play” solutions before diagnosing the business capability.
- Several commercial pages used “autopilot”, “while you sleep”, “runs itself” and similar low-oversight framing.
- Some pages made unsupported ROI or implementation-time claims.
- The AI operating-system page described a tool bundle more strongly than the organisational change behind it.
- The homepage did not clearly express the seven levels of value, seven implementation steps or organisational stack.
- The primary homepage webinar link pointed to an expired live registration rather than the replay.
- Strategy-call copy was generic and did not state the specific outcome promised in the webinar.

## Delivered changes

- Standardised the public display brand to AI Fusion and the founder name to Grant De Swardt.
- Repositioned the homepage around “Capability first. Tool second. Always.”
- Added the webinar’s seven levels of AI value and seven-step implementation method to the core journey.
- Reframed AI operating systems as organisational infrastructure spanning strategy, workflows, systems, data, people, training and governance.
- Rewrote all 49 industry pages to begin with assessment and capability rather than a predetermined tool.
- Reframed product pages as components within defined workflows, permissions and human oversight.
- Replaced unsupported ROI and timing guarantees with a measurable business-case approach based on current inefficiency.
- Updated the strategy-call promise to finding the highest-value first opportunity and safest next step.
- Aligned the webinar replay page with the seven levels, seven steps and organisational stack.
- Replaced the expired live-webinar homepage conversion path with the current replay.
- Regenerated the sitemap without changing canonical URLs.

## Preserved assets and behaviour

- Existing canonical URLs and internal-link structure
- Analytics and consent controls
- Booking and lead-capture links
- Customer reviews and their attribution
- Editorial news and blog meaning
- Privacy, terms and earnings-disclaimer substance
- Structured data, except where brand or commercial wording required alignment

## Intentional exceptions

Editorial reports and customer quotations may contain language that is outside the preferred commercial style when it is attributed or necessary to preserve the historical record. Technical identifiers, domains and email addresses retain their existing values. No customer statement was rewritten to manufacture alignment with the new position.

## Verification

- HTML inventory: 227 documents parsed.
- Static quality suite: 9 of 9 checks passed.
- Browser suite: 40 of 40 checks passed across 320px mobile, mobile, tablet, laptop and desktop profiles.
- Browser gates covered rendering, console errors, responsive overflow, keyboard access, accessibility, navigation consistency, consent gating and representative nested content.
- Internal links and local assets resolved.
- Indexed pages passed title, description, canonical and single-H1 checks.
- Sitemap contains every indexed canonical URL and excludes noindex pages.
- Repository text scans found no remaining old display-brand or founder-name variants.
- Commercial-page scans found none of the prohibited automation and guarantee phrases in scope.

## Ongoing control

`docs/canonical-messaging-standard.md` is the canonical copy standard for future AI Fusion website work. New commercial pages should be reviewed against it before release. Editorial and customer-quote exceptions should remain explicit and attributable.
