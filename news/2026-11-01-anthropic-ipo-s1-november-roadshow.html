Anthropic files for IPO — roadshow starts week of 9 November at up to £1.6 trillion valuation

The maker of Claude submitted its S-1 on 26 October and is expected to begin formal investor marketing as soon as 9 November. The listing under ticker ANT could surpass SpaceX's IPO and cement Claude as infrastructure-grade AI for business.

1 NovPublished 2026
4 minDaily brief
Impact 8/10Market signal

Anthropic filed its S-1 with the SEC on 26 October — confirming plans to list as ANT, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading the offering. The company targets a valuation between $1.5 trillion and $2 trillion, making it one of the largest technology IPOs on record.

What the S-1 shows

Anthropic's prospectus does not disclose a final valuation figure or share count — standard practice at this stage. The company raised at a $965 billion post-money valuation in its May 2026 Series H, so the $1.5–2 trillion IPO range represents a meaningful step up based on institutional investor appetite rather than a new funding event.

The roadshow marketing is expected to begin the week of 9 November, putting the first trading day before the US Thanksgiving holiday in late November. Bloomberg and the Motley Fool both report the timeline as firm unless market conditions deteriorate sharply.

The offering follows a string of significant Anthropic milestones in 2026: Claude becoming the top-ranked AI model in multiple benchmarks, the launch of the Claude Agent SDK for enterprise deployments, and the signing of large commercial contracts with the US government and several European insurers and banks.

Why it matters for UK businesses using Claude

A public Anthropic changes the commercial landscape in two specific ways. First, it brings quarterly earnings reporting — meaning pricing decisions, product roadmap, and usage terms will now be made partly with public-market expectations in mind. Second, it raises the credibility ceiling for enterprise procurement: a listed company with audited accounts and a published prospectus is easier to get approved through procurement compliance than a private firm backed by Amazon and Google.

For UK small businesses using Claude through the API or through third-party tools built on it, the near-term outlook is continuity: the roadshow period is not a product change event. The signal to watch is how Anthropic frames its commercial model in the prospectus — whether usage-based or seat-based pricing dominates the revenue model tells you a great deal about where costs will move over the next 18 months.

Coverage context

S-1 filing date confirmed by Bloomberg (26 October 2026) and Forbes. Valuation range from Bloomberg and Yahoo Finance citing sources familiar with the matter. Roadshow timing from The Motley Fool and Leverage Shares. Ticker symbol ANT confirmed in the filing. No trading date is confirmed; this depends on SEC review and market conditions.

Operator actions — three things to do now

  • Read the S-1 executive summary when it becomes freely accessible — the revenue model section will tell you how Anthropic prices usage and what commercial commitments it has made to large customers. That shapes what smaller users can expect.
  • Note your current monthly Claude API or tool spend — once a company is public, pricing reviews become more predictable (quarterly). Having a baseline figure now helps you benchmark any future changes.
  • Avoid locking into multi-year commitments on Anthropic-dependent tools until after listing — the commercial terms may shift in the twelve months post-IPO as the company adjusts its go-to-market for a public investor audience.
