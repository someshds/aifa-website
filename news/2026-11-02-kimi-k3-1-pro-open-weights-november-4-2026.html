Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights release on 4 November — 2.8 trillion parameters, 1 million token context, free API

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro releases full open model weights on 4 November 2026. The 2.8 trillion parameter model with a one million token context window is already available free via the Kimi API — here is what it means for businesses evaluating AI infrastructure costs.

Moonshot AI confirmed that full model weights for Kimi K3.1 Pro will be released on 4 November 2026, making the model fully open for download, fine-tuning, and self-hosting. The 2.8 trillion parameter mixture-of-experts model with a 1 million token context window is already live and free to use via the Kimi API.

What Kimi K3.1 Pro is

Kimi K3.1 Pro is a mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture model with 2.8 trillion total parameters and 104 billion active parameters. The 1 million token context window is one of the largest available in a free open-weight model. The model supports multimodal input and has been tested for full-project coding in Unreal, Unity, Godot, and parametric 3D modelling in Blender and CAD tools.

Moonshot AI also released K3.1 Pro Highspeed — a companion variant running at 2–3× the output speed — alongside the base model.

The responsible-AI step worth noting

Before releasing open weights, Moonshot AI deliberately identified and removed offensive cybersecurity capability data from both training stages — retaining general-purpose coding capabilities but reducing the model's potential for use in security exploits. This mirrors what Mistral and other frontier open-weight model developers have done, and is increasingly the expected standard for large open-weight releases.

Why it matters for UK businesses

The open-weight release expands your options in three ways. First, it is free to use via the Kimi API right now — meaning you can test it against your own use case with no commitment. Second, the 1 million token context window makes it viable for tasks that paid API models truncate: long document processing, full codebase review, large-scale data extraction. Third, open weights mean you can self-host it if your use case requires data to stay on your infrastructure — relevant for businesses handling sensitive client data.

The practical value for most small UK businesses is not self-hosting — it is having another free, capable model to benchmark against your current paid usage. If Kimi K3.1 Pro handles 70% of your workload at zero marginal cost, your AI infrastructure spend changes materially.

Operator actions — three things to do now

  • Test Kimi K3.1 Pro on your most common AI task at kimi.ai — the 1M context means you can upload large documents without chunking. Compare output quality to your current tool.
  • If you're evaluating AI infrastructure costs — add free open-weight models to your comparison. The cost gap between paid APIs and capable free models is now significant enough to affect business case calculations.
  • Note the open-weight date is 4 November — if your use case requires self-hosting for data residency or compliance reasons, Kimi K3.1 Pro may be worth evaluating once weights are publicly downloadable.
