What happened

Why it matters for UK SMB / service operators

Many UK service firms use chatbots, AI email drafts, or AI marketing creatives for EU clients. Map where AI touches customers, check vendor contracts for who owns disclosure, and label AI-facing touchpoints clearly.

Operator move
