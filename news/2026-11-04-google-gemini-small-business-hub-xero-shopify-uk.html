Google Gemini's small business hub now includes UK — Xero, Shopify, Canva integrations rolling out for all users

Google launched a dedicated business landing page in the Gemini app on 20 October, with connections to Xero, Shopify, Canva, Adobe, Dropbox, Wix, and Zoho. The EEA and UK exclusion from June's Google Business Profile integration has been lifted in the October update. Available to all Gemini app users at no extra cost.

Google's Gemini app now has a dedicated small business landing page with new integrations covering Xero, Shopify, Canva, Dropbox, Wix, Zoho, and Google's own Workspace and Ads tools. The June EEA and UK access restriction has been lifted in the October 20 update. The hub is available to all Gemini app users at no extra cost — including users on the free tier.

What the Gemini small business hub offers

The hub is a central landing page inside the Gemini app that surfaces business-relevant tools, recently connected apps, and financial and marketing workflows. Google describes it as a business operating surface rather than a standard AI chatbot — the idea is that you open Gemini as the first screen in your working day, not search.

The new third-party integrations are the most practically useful addition:

  • Xero — connect your Xero account and ask Gemini to summarise unpaid invoices, flag overdue debtors, or pull a 30-day cash flow snapshot without opening a spreadsheet.
  • Shopify — ask Gemini to summarise orders, flag returns, or identify your top-selling products over any period.
  • Canva — generate a Canva design brief from a Gemini conversation and open it directly in Canva, maintaining your brand style across generated assets.
  • Google Ads — query campaign performance and get Gemini to draft copy variations tested against your existing headline data.
  • Google Business Profile — respond to reviews and update business information directly from Gemini (the June EEA/UK restriction on this specific feature is now also lifted).

Financial insights is a separate capability: upload receipts or share a spreadsheet link and Gemini will extract totals, categorise expenses, and produce an interactive financial summary. This is aimed at the significant proportion of UK micro-businesses that do not use dedicated accounting software.

Who benefits most

The Gemini small business hub is most useful for businesses already on Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Drive) and using at least one of the connected apps. For a business on Gmail + Xero + Google Ads — a common combination for UK service businesses — the hub effectively becomes a lightweight operations dashboard requiring no additional software spend.

Businesses not on Google Workspace who use Shopify as their primary customer-facing tool will find the Shopify integration useful but limited without the broader Workspace data context. In that case, Gemini is acting as a faster query interface on Shopify data — useful, but not transformative.

The free-tier availability is significant. Many UK micro-businesses run entirely on free Google accounts. The business hub puts AI-assisted financial and operations queries within reach without requiring a Workspace subscription upgrade.

The UK access question

Google's June 10 announcement of the Business Profile integration included a footnote: "Excluding the EEA and UK." The October 20 update does not include that footnote. Google has not issued a specific statement about the UK restriction being lifted, but the absence of the exclusion in the October update — which covers the Business Profile feature as well as the new third-party integrations — is taken by industry sources as confirmation that UK users now have full access.

The June restriction was most likely a data-sharing compliance issue tied to the Google Business Profile connection specifically, rather than the AI features more broadly. The ICO's agentic AI call for evidence is the ongoing regulatory process that Google, OpenAI, and other providers are navigating concurrently.

Operator actions

  • If you use Xero — open the Gemini app now, navigate to the business hub, and connect your Xero account. Ask Gemini: "What invoices are overdue and total more than £500?" as a first test. That query alone saves 15–20 minutes of manual review weekly.
  • If you use Shopify — connect your store and ask for a 7-day orders summary. Use this as your morning operations check to replace manually logging in to the Shopify admin.
  • If you use Google Ads — ask Gemini to summarise your last 30 days of ad performance and suggest the three most-improved ad groups. Use this as briefing notes before any agency review call.
  • If you are not yet on Google Workspace — this hub is an additional reason to consolidate on Workspace if you're evaluating platforms. The integration depth is currently stronger here than on comparable Microsoft 365 Business Basic tiers.
