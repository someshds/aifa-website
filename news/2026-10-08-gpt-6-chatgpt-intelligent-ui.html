GPT-6 arrives in everyday ChatGPT

OpenAI is bringing GPT-6 to ChatGPT's Chat tab, with Intelligent UI answers that can include charts, buttons, forms and small tools, reaching free users from 8 October.

8 OctPublished 2026
4 minDaily brief
Impact 9/10News signal

AI is moving into the tools people already use. Interactive answers with charts, buttons and forms; paid tiers from 7 Oct, Free and Go from 8 Oct; rollout varies by account.

What OpenAI is rolling out

On Wednesday 7 October, OpenAI said it is bringing a new GPT-6 model to ChatGPT's Chat tab, which it says is used by more than 1.2 billion people each week. It comes with Intelligent UI: responses that can include graphics, tappable buttons, forms, charts and interactive tools inside the conversation. OpenAI's examples include a Sunday roast planner with an adjustable guest count and a bill splitter.

The rollout started on 7 October for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise, and expands to Free and Go from 8 October. Tom's Guide reports paid tiers get GPT-6 Sol and Free and Go get the lighter GPT-6 Luna. Enterprise availability depends on workplace admin settings. OpenAI also says GPT-6 Instant starts answering web-search questions 44% sooner on average than GPT-5.6 Instant — when the answer begins, not how fast it finishes.

Who sees it, and when

This is the first GPT-6 access most ChatGPT users will get. The rollout is account by account, so it may not show for everyone at once.

Coverage context

GPT-6 is landing inside the Chat tab people already open, with answers that can include charts, buttons and forms. Access is moving from paid plans on 7 October to Free and Go from 8 October, still varying by account.
