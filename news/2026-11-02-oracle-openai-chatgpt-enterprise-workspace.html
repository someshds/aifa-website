Oracle brings ChatGPT directly into business apps — NetSuite, Fusion, and OCI get AI built in

Oracle AI Workspace integrates ChatGPT with Oracle enterprise applications including NetSuite, Oracle Fusion Applications, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. The era of switching between your business software and an AI tab is ending.

2 NovPublished 2026
4 minDaily brief
Impact 6/10Tool signal

On 27 October 2026, Oracle and OpenAI announced Oracle AI Workspace at Oracle AI World in Las Vegas — a direct integration of ChatGPT and Codex into Oracle's enterprise applications, databases, and cloud infrastructure, embedding AI inside the tools businesses already use rather than sitting alongside them.

🔊Listen to this article

What Oracle AI Workspace does

Oracle AI Workspace connects ChatGPT to Oracle Fusion Applications, NetSuite, Oracle AI Database, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) via plugins in the existing ChatGPT interface. Employees retrieve enterprise information, complete approved workflows, and interact with Oracle systems without leaving the ChatGPT window — while maintaining existing security and governance controls.

Oracle also announced App Composer, which uses Codex to allow employees to create enterprise applications without traditional coding expertise. Oracle handles deployment, infrastructure, and operational controls on the back end.

The announcement was made at Oracle AI World in Las Vegas, where Oracle positioned AI Workspace as a productivity multiplier for the 400,000-plus organisations running Oracle software globally.

Why it matters for UK businesses using Oracle tools

UK businesses using NetSuite for accounting, ERP, and inventory management represent a large slice of the SME market — NetSuite is one of the most widely adopted cloud ERP platforms for businesses in the £500k–£50m revenue range. Oracle AI Workspace means AI capability will arrive inside the software these businesses are already paying for, rather than requiring a separate AI tool layer or context-switching between systems.

The integration is not a cosmetic feature. Connecting ChatGPT to live enterprise data — including Oracle Fusion's HR, finance, and procurement modules — means AI can act on real operational data rather than general knowledge. That changes the quality of answers and the scope of actions AI can take.

Coverage context

Partnership announced 27 October 2026 at Oracle AI World, Las Vegas. Details from GuruFocus and Channel Insider reporting on the official announcement. App Composer details confirmed in the Oracle press release. No UK-specific rollout date has been confirmed — Oracle typically phases enterprise feature availability by region.

Operator actions — three things to do now

  • If your business uses NetSuite or Oracle Fusion — ask your Oracle account manager or NetSuite partner for the AI Workspace rollout timeline and whether your tier includes it.
  • If you're evaluating ERP or cloud software — embedded AI is now a standard selection criterion, not a premium differentiator. Factor it into any vendor comparison you're running in 2026 or 2027.
  • Watch the governance conditions — Oracle AI Workspace claims to maintain existing security controls. Before enabling any enterprise AI integration, confirm which data leaves your Oracle environment and under what terms.
