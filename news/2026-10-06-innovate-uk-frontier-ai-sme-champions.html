Innovate UK opens £3.1m Frontier AI fund for SMEs

UKRI published the Frontier AI: SME Champions, phase 1 opportunity, with UK-registered micro, small and medium-sized businesses able to apply for a share of up to £3.1 million.

The question is moving from what AI can do to how anyone can check it. UK-registered SMEs can apply alone for feasibility funding in frontier AI and machine learning. Closes 11:00am UK time, 11 November 2026.

Who can apply, and by when

UKRI published the Frontier AI: SME Champions, phase 1 opportunity on 5 October (it opened 1 October). UK-registered micro, small and medium-sized businesses can apply, as single applicants, for a share of up to £3.1 million for feasibility studies in frontier AI and machine learning "with a clear route to defensible scale-up." It closes at 11:00am UK time on 11 November 2026.

This is for SMEs building AI technology, not for firms adopting off-the-shelf tools.

Coverage context

A £3.1 million feasibility fund is now open for UK-registered SMEs developing frontier AI, closing at 11:00am UK time on 11 November 2026.
