Who this is for

Owner-led small and service businesses where too much still depends on the owner. It usually sounds like this:

  • Enquiries wait until the evening for a reply, and some never get one
  • Quotes, follow-ups and admin rely on you remembering
  • The team has tried ChatGPT, but nothing about how the work gets done has changed
  • You pay for software you barely use

If that’s you, the fix is rarely “more AI”. It’s picking the right piece of work and making it run without you.

How working together goes

  1. 1
    Assess

    Look at how work actually flows today: enquiries, quotes, follow-ups, admin, data and the people doing it.

  2. 2
    Prioritise

    Choose the one or two changes worth doing first, weighed against time saved, cost and risk.

  3. 3
    Build

    Set the workflow up around the tools you already use, with a person approving the steps where judgement matters.

  4. 4
    Train

    Your team learns it on their real working day, so it sticks after the consultant has gone.

If the answer turns out to be better data, a simpler process or ordinary automation rather than AI, Grant will tell you.
