What a good AI consultant should actually do

Ask many AI consulting firms a question and you get a tool recommendation. The order should be the other way round: capability first, tool second. A useful AI consultant should:

  • Understand how your business really runs (enquiries, admin, handoffs, systems and data) before suggesting anything
  • Find the single highest-value place to begin, judged on return, speed, risk and team readiness
  • Design around the tools you already use, with permissions, oversight and clear escalation
  • Train your team so the change sticks, rather than leaving a system nobody trusts
  • Tell you plainly when AI isn’t the answer and a simpler process fix would be better

How our AI consultancy works: seven steps, in order

Every stage protects the next one from expensive guessing.

  1. 1
    Assess

    Understand the business model, goals, bottlenecks, workflows, systems, data, team, risks and opportunities.

  2. 2

  3. 3

Timelines and outcomes follow the assessment. They depend on scope, systems, data, risk and team readiness, so we don’t quote a one-size-fits-all implementation time.

AI consultant FAQs

What does an AI consultant do?

An AI consultant helps a business decide where AI will genuinely add value, then plans and supports the rollout. At AI Fusion that means assessing your workflows, systems, data and team; prioritising the highest-value starting point; designing and implementing it; and training people to use it well.

How much does an AI consultant cost in the UK?

It depends on scope, so we don’t publish a fixed consulting fee. The first opportunity call is free. Workshops are priced once we understand the issue. If you only need the operating platform (CRM, booking, inbox and automations), platform seats start from £97 a month; see pricing.

Do I need to be technical to work with an AI consultancy?

No. We explain things in plain English and handle the technical design. Your input is how the business actually runs: where work gets stuck, what matters to customers and where a person must stay in control.

Will AI replace my team?

That isn’t the aim. Agents and automations take on defined, repetitive work with permissions, oversight and escalation. Leadership, judgement, relationships and accountability stay with people, and training is part of every rollout.

Do you only work with businesses in Sussex?

No. AI Fusion is based in East Sussex. Grant meets clients in person in East Grinstead and works with businesses across the UK on Zoom.

What’s the difference between an AI consultant and an AI automation agency?

An AI automation agency usually starts with the build. An AI consultant starts with the decision: what capability you need, where to begin and what it will touch. AI Fusion does both, in that order: assess first, then design, build and train.
