What the hour covers

You will see a founder priority inbox on a sanitised mailbox, not a tour of a finished product.

  • The few messages that need a decision, instead of the whole inbox
  • Why each one is in that queue
  • The original email, before you do anything
  • Which business or subject it belongs to, when more than one thing is running
  • A draft that stays a draft until you send it

What you leave with

You leave knowing what that inbox should show you. You do not leave with a finished app. Building it is a different session.
