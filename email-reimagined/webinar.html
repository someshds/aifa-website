What we cover

Three belief shifts, not a list of productivity tips. The free hour does not hand you a finished app. Building it is the Email Re-imagined course.

The inbox is not a to-do list.

A newsletter, a complaint, a receipt, a lead, a vendor question, and an order issue sit as peers. The problem is decision discovery, not only volume.

Filters solve routing, not judgment.

Labels, folders, stars, and rules can still leave the founder inspecting the inbox. The mechanism: email, then evaluate, explain the priority, surface the decision, prepare a draft, and the human decides.

Automation does not mean giving up control.

The system prepares, the founder reviews, the founder decides. A suggested reply stays a draft. Send waits for you.
