October 2026

1.1.306 and 1.1.307

Automatic version · 9 October 2026

  • The version file reads 1.1.307. No separate owner-facing note was written for 1.1.306 or 1.1.307.

1.1.305 — The last verified inbox stays up during a Gmail cooldown

Automatic version · 9 October 2026 · The matching work note is dated this day. The heading in the app’s change file had no date of its own.

  • A Gmail quota error keeps the last verified inbox on screen, with a warning and the retry delay.
  • The saved inbox is reused for 30 minutes. A full refresh runs hourly.
  • A second manual Refresh within five minutes reuses that saved inbox.
  • On startup, the saved inbox stays visible while a due Gmail check runs in the background.

1.1.303 — Priority Inbox lists every qualifying message it has loaded

Automatic version · 9 October 2026

  • The inbox load asks for up to 600 current messages.
  • Priority Inbox lists every loaded message that scores 34 or higher and has not been set aside, ranked by score.
  • New mail, including mail first labelled as marketing, goes through the deeper scoring pass.
  • During a temporary Google limit, the previous verified snapshot can stay on screen while the wider load retries.

1.1.294 — Reconnect Gmail from the app

Automatic version · 7 October 2026

  • When Gmail says its authorisation has expired or been revoked, the banner shows Re-authorise Gmail.
  • That opens the Google consent flow in the browser. Check connection then refreshes the inbox.
  • Other temporary Gmail failures still show Retry.

1.1.287 — Reply suggestions follow the email

Automatic version · 7 October 2026

  • Suggest replies reads the full email before drafting.
  • You get three distinct options: a sensible yes, one useful question, and a polite decline or not-now.
  • Choosing an option places it in the editable draft. Confirm send is the step that sends.

1.1.283 — A short Google failure keeps the inbox on screen

Automatic version · 6 October 2026

  • A short-lived Google precondition error is retried once.
  • If that still fails, the app shows the last verified inbox for up to seven days and labels it Showing last verified inbox.
  • A failed refresh leaves the inbox you already have on screen and explains the failure.

1.1.280 — Archive finishes in the connected inbox first

Automatic version · 6 October 2026

  • Archive completes in the connected Gmail inbox first.
  • If a matching copy still needs tidying, that tidy-up is queued, saved locally, and retried. The app says when it is still pending.
  • Archive, spam, and completion can be undone where Gmail supports it.
  • A timeout checks Gmail before the app offers another try.

1.1.274 — Clearer recovery after a Gmail timeout

Automatic version · 5 October 2026

  • After a Gmail action times out, the app checks Gmail before another action is allowed. If the live result cannot be confirmed, actions pause.

1.0.2 — Refresh and Load older

Sidebar version · 4 October 2026 · Updated 4 Oct 2026

  • Refresh loads inbox mail from the last 90 days, up to 250 messages.
  • Load older loads inbox mail from the last year, up to 600 messages.
  • New rows are scored. Rows already scored are left as they are.
  • On this sidebar release, Priority Inbox is still the top 10 messages scored 34 or higher.

1.0.1 — Version on the sidebar

Sidebar version · 4 October 2026

  • The sidebar shows the version and the updated date. Later edits bump the patch and set that date to the day of the change.

Drafts, Trash, and Schedule

4 October 2026 · No version number was recorded. These were in place before sidebar 1.0.1 was shown.

  • The window title is Email Re-imagined.
  • Draft reply can start from a yes, a request for details, or not right now. Suggest replies and Improve draft stay inside facts already in the email or the draft.
  • Send draft asks you to confirm. Confirm send is the step that sends immediately.
  • Unsubscribe & delete moves the message to Trash. Undo can restore it from Trash.
  • Schedule asks for a date and time, stores the draft, and sends it then. Cancel removes that draft before the time.

1.1.272 — OpenAI mail stays high priority

Automatic version · 3 October 2026

  • Mail whose sender is OpenAI, including its subdomains, stays in the high-priority list.
  • Lookalike sender domains stay out. A priority you set for a sender yourself still wins.

Inbox, Priority, Archive, Snooze, and the Mac window

3 October 2026 · No version number was recorded. Sidebar numbering started the next day at 1.0.1.

  • The inbox lists recent messages, with a sidebar, top bar, summary chips, message list, reading pane, and side panel.
  • Priority Inbox shows the top 10 messages scored 34 or higher.
  • Archive and Complete & remove take the message out of the inbox. Undo can put it back.
  • Open original opens that thread in the browser.
  • Snooze takes the message out of the inbox and adds a Snoozed label. It stays there until you undo it.
  • The list can load up to 100 messages.
  • A Mac app window opens the same screen.
