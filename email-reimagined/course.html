Who this is for

You run the business. The inbox is eating the week. You do not need another person to sort mail for you, and you are not asking AI Fusion to build the whole thing yet. You want a clear way to see the few messages that need a decision, and to keep a person in charge of what gets sent.

What you work through

The course follows the same founder priority inbox the live hour demonstrates on a sanitised mailbox.

  • How to surface the few messages that need a decision, instead of living in the whole inbox
  • How to see why a message is in that queue
  • How to keep the original email in view before you do anything
  • How to tell which business or subject a message belongs to when more than one thing is running
  • How a draft stays a draft until you send it
